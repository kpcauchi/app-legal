Aisly · Data controls

Request account and data deletion

If you created an Aisly family account using Google Sign-In, you can ask for that account and associated personal data to be deleted even if the app is no longer installed.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to kpcdigitalstudio@gmail.com with the subject Aisly account deletion request.
  2. Preferably send it from the Google email address you used to sign in to Aisly, so the correct account can be identified. If that is not possible, explain which account you used.
  3. State that you want your Aisly account and associated personal data deleted. We may request reasonable verification of account ownership; never send passwords or verification codes.

Email a deletion request

What happens to your information

Following verification, the account's authentication information and personal family-membership records can be removed from Aisly's services. Shopping content shared with other family members may still be needed by that workspace. We'll explain any such information, any necessary exceptions, and what can be removed from the shared workspace as part of handling your request.

Uninstalling Aisly or signing out does not, by itself, delete your cloud account. Removing the app from your device can remove locally saved data, depending on your Android settings.
