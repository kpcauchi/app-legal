Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

Aisly is an Android app for organising shopping lists and, if you choose, sharing them with members of a family workspace. This policy explains what information is handled when you use the app, who can see it, and how to request its deletion.

Who operates Aisly

Aisly is independently developed and operated. For privacy questions or requests, contact kpcdigitalstudio@gmail.com.

What data Aisly handles

  • Google Sign-In information: if you sign in, Google and Firebase Authentication process your account identity, including your unique account identifier, email address and display name as made available by Google. Aisly stores your Firebase user identifier and display name in family membership records.
  • Family workspace information: family name, membership role, invitations and associated timestamps; family members may see other members' display names and roles.
  • Shopping information: shops, categories, aisles, catalogue items, item quantities, shopping statuses, notes, optional item prices and currencies, and links between products in different shops.
  • On-device information: locally saved shopping data, app preferences and cached copies of family data used to keep previously loaded lists available offline.
  • Photos shared by you: when you choose to attach or take a photo for Android's share action, Aisly prepares that photo locally for the app you select. Aisly does not upload those shared photos to its own Firebase Storage backend.

You can use local shopping lists without signing in. Family sharing and multi-device synchronisation require Google Sign-In.

Why the data is used

The information is used to authenticate your session, create and manage family membership, organise shopping lists, synchronise changes between authorised devices, maintain offline availability, and provide the functions you request. Aisly does not currently include a third-party advertising or analytics SDK and does not sell shopping or account information.

Who can access or receive data

  • Your family workspace: the other signed-in family members you or the family owner have admitted can access and edit shared shopping content and view membership information.
  • Google and Firebase: Aisly uses Google Sign-In, Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore to process authentication and store/synchronise shared information. Google's applicable privacy information is available at Google Privacy Policy.
  • Apps you choose to share with: when you use Android Share, content such as a shopping item, note or selected photo is sent to the destination app you select. That app's privacy practices then apply.

Security

Network connections to Google/Firebase use encrypted transport. Shared shopping records are protected by Firebase security rules that restrict access according to family membership. Local cached information is kept on your device under Android's app storage protections. No online system can guarantee absolute security.

How long information is kept

Shopping and family data are retained while the relevant family workspace is active or until an authorised user deletes them or a verified deletion request is processed. Sign-out by itself does not delete stored family data. Local copies and caches may remain on a device until app data is cleared or the app is uninstalled. Service-provider backups may persist temporarily under Google's backup and retention processes. Where information must be retained for a lawful or security-related reason, the reason will be explained in response to a deletion request.

Your choices and deletion requests

You may manage or delete individual shopping entries and shops using the app's available controls. Family owners can remove family members. To request deletion of your Aisly account and associated personal data, follow the Aisly account deletion instructions or email kpcdigitalstudio@gmail.com from the email address associated with your account.

We may need to verify ownership before acting on a deletion request. When an account is deleted, its authentication and personal membership information must be removed. Because shopping lists belong to a shared family workspace, collaborative list contents may require separate handling to avoid deleting information belonging to other members. We will explain any necessary distinctions and permissible retention when processing a request.

Children

Aisly is a general shopping-list tool for families. It is not specifically designed for children, and children should not be given a sign-in account without any permissions and supervision required by their parent or guardian and applicable Google account rules.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as Aisly changes. The latest version and its effective date will be published on this page. Material changes to data processing may require additional notice or user choices.
