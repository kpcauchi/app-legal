Request deletion of Zenvuno data

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Zenvuno is an IPTV player that uses a provider chosen by the user. The current version does not create a Zenvuno account or store your IPTV login in a developer-operated cloud account. You can clear app data from your Android device, and you can contact the developer about personal information you may have voluntarily sent to support.

Request deletion from the developer

To request deletion of personal information held by Zenvuno support, send an email to kpcdigitalstudio@gmail.com with the subject Zenvuno data deletion request. Briefly describe the information you want removed, such as a previous support conversation, and provide enough context to locate it. Do not include your IPTV password, playlist tokens or parental PIN. We may ask for reasonable verification before deleting information.

Email a deletion request

We will review requests and respond within a reasonable period, taking account of applicable legal requirements.

Delete data stored on your Android device

  1. Open your device's Settings.
  2. Go to Apps → Zenvuno → Storage (the wording may differ by device).
  3. Choose Clear storage or Clear data to erase the app's locally stored information. Uninstalling Zenvuno also removes its app-private data through Android's normal app removal process.

This removes locally stored IPTV provider connection details, cached catalogues, favourites, hidden content, viewing history, parental settings and other saved app preferences on that device. Clearing data cannot be undone unless you previously saved an encrypted backup.

What is deleted and what may remain

  • Local Zenvuno data: remains until you clear app storage or uninstall the app; the developer cannot remotely erase app-private data from your device.
  • Exported backups: encrypted backup files that you saved outside the app remain under your control and must be deleted from their storage location separately. Copies held by the destination service may follow that service's retention policy.
  • IPTV provider information: viewing activity, subscription accounts or records held by your chosen external provider cannot be deleted by Zenvuno. Contact that provider directly.
  • Support correspondence: personal details you voluntarily sent to the developer can be requested for deletion by email. Such information may be retained only as needed to respond to the request or meet applicable legal or security obligations.

There is no fixed automatic 90-day deletion schedule for device-local data, exported backup files or information held by third-party providers. Any legally required retention of support correspondence will be explained when responding to a request.
