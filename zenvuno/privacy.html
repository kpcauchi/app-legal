Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Zenvuno is an IPTV player for Android phones and compatible TVs. You connect your own IPTV provider or playlist. Zenvuno does not supply channels, movies, series, subscriptions or streaming content.

Contact

For privacy enquiries, email kpcdigitalstudio@gmail.com.

Information handled by Zenvuno

  • Provider details: server address, username and password for Xtream connections, or playlist and optional EPG URLs for M3U connections. URLs can contain access tokens.
  • App settings and content history: favourites, hidden items, recently watched content, playback preferences, Kids Mode settings, parental PIN and related preferences.
  • Provider catalogue: channel, movie and series metadata retrieved from the service you choose, which may be cached on your device.
  • User-created backup files: when requested, a password-encrypted export of your settings, including provider connection details.
  • Support messages: information you voluntarily send when contacting support.

How information is used and stored

Provider details are used to connect to your chosen IPTV service, retrieve catalogues and access streams. The app stores provider connection details in encrypted app-private storage using Android Keystore. Other settings and viewing preferences are stored locally on your device. You do not need a Zenvuno account to use the current app. Zenvuno currently has no developer-operated cloud account or automatic cloud backup of settings, and does not contain an advertising SDK or developer-operated analytics service.

Third parties

  • Your IPTV provider: receives connection requests, relevant credentials or playlist tokens, and media requests as needed to provide your chosen content. Its own privacy policy applies.
  • Google Cast: if you choose to cast, Google Cast and your selected receiver handle playback metadata and stream addresses needed to play content. These addresses can contain provider tokens. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Your selected backup destination: if you export a backup, it is saved to the storage location or other app you select using Android's file picker. The destination's practices apply.

Zenvuno does not sell personal information or operate a service that receives your saved provider credentials.

Connection and backup security

HTTPS is recommended. Some IPTV services require HTTP, which is unencrypted and may expose credentials, tokens and streaming data to anyone with access to the network path. Password-encrypted backups are created only at your request. Keep the files and their passwords secure.

Retention and deletion

Local preferences and cached data remain on your device until removed through available in-app controls, Android's clear-storage option or uninstallation. You control any exported backup files and must delete them separately from the location you chose. Your IPTV provider may retain its own records according to its policies.

To clear Zenvuno data, open Android Settings, find Apps > Zenvuno > Storage, and choose Clear storage (wording varies by device). You may also uninstall the app. For questions or privacy requests contact kpcdigitalstudio@gmail.com.

Children and Kids Mode

Kids Mode can help restrict content identified as adult, but classification may be incomplete. Parents should review their IPTV provider's content and supervise children as appropriate.

Policy updates

This policy may change if the app's features or data practices change. Updates and their effective date will appear on this page.
